# Create a Soulseek account

You need a Soulseek **network** username and password. A login for the Soulseek website or forum is separate.

1. Download SoulseekQt from the [official Soulseek download page](https://www.slsknet.org/news/node/1) and open it.
2. At its login prompt, choose a new username and a password used only for Soulseek. You can use a nickname; do not put your email address or real name in the username.
3. Connect. An unused username is registered when you successfully log in; there is no separate network sign-up form. If your chosen name is already taken and you receive an incorrect-password message, choose another name. For an existing account, enter its original password instead.
4. Save your credentials privately, then disconnect or close SoulseekQt before using the same account in FLAC-Downloader. Two clients using one account can disconnect each other.
5. Open FLAC-Downloader and enter those credentials in the **Soulseek account** panel. Import a tracklist and start a download when ready. Opening this app alone does not connect or download.

If you already use Nicotine+ or SoulseekQt, use your existing network credentials after disconnecting that client. You do not need to keep another client running.

## Login trouble

- **Incorrect password:** check spelling and capitalization. For a new account, try a different username rather than repeatedly retrying a taken name.
- **Disconnected by another login:** close the other client, or use a separate account for this app.
- **Cannot connect:** check your connection and the [official Soulseek FAQ](https://www.slsknet.org/news/faq-page). A connection failure does not necessarily mean your password is wrong.
- **Old account no longer works:** the official FAQ says usernames without privileges can be recycled after 30 days without a login. Your former name may now belong to someone else.

Never include your password, private settings, or real tracklists in a GitHub issue. Share only files you are entitled to share, and never share personal document folders.

Sources: [Soulseek download](https://www.slsknet.org/news/node/1), [Soulseek FAQ](https://www.slsknet.org/news/faq-page), and [Soulseek technical discussion on new accounts](https://groups.google.com/g/soulseek-discussion/c/0BPcTnGWg80).
